Current fresh training uses motion_data_protocol=hiking_walk_v1 and
sensor_protocol=hiking_depth_only_v1. It adapts the official complete walking
file selection, bilinear motion interpolation, forward-difference velocity
calculation, sagittal symmetry, 67-dimensional AMP observations over ten frames,
and independent standing resets. The AMP discriminator loss and update adapt
Instinct RL's Wasabi implementation. AMP inputs omit the former local ground
height proxies and running normalization. Depth learning uses eight depth-only
channels; raw validity remains diagnostic and is excluded from learned vision.

The hiking_full_v1 task additionally adapts all26 ordinary-G1 rewards,
eight-sample term-major proprioception, source ideal-PD actuator parameters
and reset-sampled delays, terrain-specific commands/tracking curriculum,
and the complete ten-family source terrain arrays at5cm. Sharp-edge
extraction, greedy edge merging and virtual-cylinder penetration adapt
sensors/volume_points and terrains/virtual_obstacle/edge_cylinder.py.
The physical MuJoCo surface uses heightfield triangles and rotated convex
boxes; it cannot reproduce PhysX's slope-threshold vertex displacement,
two-coefficient friction law, restitution or solver joint-velocity limit.
These engine limitations are recorded explicitly. Torso resets/observations
are transformed through FK into the local pelvis-root model. The historical
walking task retains its original coarser collision fields and task settings.
Training version 3 uses the ordinary unshod G1ParkourRoughEnvCfg. Its original
g1_29dof_torsobase_popsicle.urdf and head_link.STL are included under
src/assets/robots/unitree_g1; the head is named hiking_head_link.STL there.
Both are from source/instinctlab/instinctlab/assets/resources/unitree_g1 in
the InstinctLab commit above (urdf/ and meshes/, respectively). The local
adapter re-roots this asset at the pelvis for GenCoord and converts the source
cylinders to capsules as requested by the original URDF importer.
World-model, Actor and Critic
updates follow SuperMimic, while the visual latent follows InstinctMJ. The
walking task has no get-up objective. These are documented framework and model
adaptations, not a complete reproduction of the original Hiking PPO pipeline.

Historical filtered_walk_turn_v2 and local_depth_valid_v1 checkpoints retain
their saved filtering, ground-height features, camera and validity-channel
semantics. Earlier joint walking/get-up experiments remain available only with
their explicit historical task and checkpoint configuration.

Published training source does not include IsaacLab, author checkpoints or the
full Hiking motion archive. Three unmodified official motion source files are
bundled under src/assets/motions/g1/hiking_official, with upstream attribution
and fixed file digests. They populate an ignored local cache without a network
request. Code attribution does not establish separate terms for motion data.
